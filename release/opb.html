AFPC Acronym Reference & Rules

Bundled offline lookup uses the October 28, 2024 AFPC individually approved list (148 entries). Highlighted terms are local review items only.

AFPC separately permits common ranks, office symbols, squadron-and-above organizations, weapons/platforms, and standard symbols or measurements by category. Custom acronyms suppress local reminders without changing the official lookup.

At least one performance statement is expected per assessment section; white space is allowed. The reference tool permits “THIS SECTION NOT USED.” Limits and historical guidance come from OPB Prep Tool v1.5.pdf (internally labeled v1.4, 29 Aug 2023), with Mission ALQ guidance corrected to official Air Force Mission qualities.
